## CIA, "CIA's Role in the Study of UFOs, 1947-90" (Gerald Haines, 1997)
PDF: !CIA Role in the Study of UFOs 1947-90.pdf · https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/cia-role-study-UFOs.pdf
- Covers the Jan 1953 Robertson Panel (physicist H.P. Robertson): worried about reports clogging channels in the Cold War; recommended a public "education" / debunking campaign (media, even Disney) and monitoring civilian UFO groups. Documented debunking policy.
- Also claims over half of 1950s-60s sightings were U-2/A-12 spy flights and the Air Force misled the public to protect them.

## Atomic Energy Act of 1954 (as amended)
PDF: !Atomic Energy Act of 1954 (as amended).pdf · definitions: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/2014
- Grusch (via the video) says "special nuclear material" covers any material releasing atomic energy, so crash material gets nuclear-level secrecy. The actual text (42 USC 2014(aa)) names plutonium and enriched uranium plus "any other material which the Commission ... determines to be special nuclear material": a discretionary door, not the broad wording claimed.
- "Restricted data" (2014(y)) is classified at birth ("born secret") without anyone stamping it. That is the stronger mechanism for the secrecy argument.
