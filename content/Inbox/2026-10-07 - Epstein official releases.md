# Epstein official releases through 2026-10-07

No single official "complete Epstein email" set exists.

- House Oversight estate productions: Sept 8 (358 pages, birthday book), Sept 12 second set, Sept 26 partial third batch (8,544 documents received, not all posted), Nov 12 additional 20,000 pages. Sept 2 was 33,295 DOJ pages posted by the House, not estate mail. Nov 18 Comer said ~65,000 pages released in total. Later 2026 House posts are interview transcripts, not another email dump.
- DOJ Epstein Files Transparency Act (PL 119-38, signed 2025-11-19): library at https://www.justice.gov/epstein. Dec 19 2025 first production (hundreds of thousands of pages; data sets 1–7). Dec 23 about 30,000 more pages (data set 8, third-party dating). Jan 30 2026: over 3 million more pages, 2,000+ videos, 180,000 images; total nearly 3.5 million pages. Data sets 9–12 are the bulk. DOJ collected 6 million+ potentially responsive pages; ~200,000 withheld or redacted for privilege. The rest of the gap is duplicates, statutory exceptions, and unrelated material. No clean split published.
- Pulled and reposted: Feb 2 2026 several thousand; early Feb ~9,500; March 2 47,635 files down for victim redaction. DOJ said files were not deleted. A citizen audit counted ~68,000 removed and said they were restored by March 2026 with size changes. Library page stamp last updated 2026-09-11; what changed is unknown.
- Still contested as of 2026-10-07: Phang v. Blanche (Sullivan, D.D.C.) on specific redactions; Democracy Defenders Fund v. DOJ (Kollar-Kotelly, 2026-09-30) expedites FOIA, does not release files; Wyden says at least one FBI 302 of a banker interview is still unreleased.
- Search: cite justice.gov/epstein or the House Drive/Dropbox, not Jmail. Jmail (Riley Walz, Luke Igel) indexes House Gmail PDFs, DOJ files, and a separate Yahoo cache (Bloomberg authenticated a similar mailbox in Sept 2025; DDoSecrets/Drop Site published vetted batches). Jemini answers are not evidence. Yahoo material is not an official release.
