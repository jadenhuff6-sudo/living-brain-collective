# CIA declassified UFO files and disclosure archives

## Result

**Verified collection:** The Black Vault's CIA UFO CD-ROM release is saved locally in its untouched and searchable forms. The untouched archive has 713 multi-page image files containing **2,780 original pages** and 713 accompanying text files. The converted archive has **713 searchable PDFs and 3,493 PDF pages**: the same 2,780 pages plus one Black Vault cover page per record. Both ZIPs passed internal integrity checks, and the per-PDF manifest records page counts and SHA-256 hashes. This is a defined CIA release, **not every CIA declassified file or every UAP record**. [The Black Vault's release and FOIA case F-2020-02272](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/ufos-the-central-intelligence-agency-cia-collection/).

**Person:** There are two credible matches. **John Greenewald Jr.** fits “the person who gets declassified documents”: he founded The Black Vault and obtained the CIA CD-ROM. **Steven Greer** fits the literal phrase *disclosure documents*: he founded the distinct Disclosure Project Intelligence Archive. The spoken physical description cannot verify either identity. The two archives and their claims should remain separate. [Greenewald's collection](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/ufos-the-central-intelligence-agency-cia-collection/) · [Greer's archive announcement](https://drstevengreer.com/dpia/).

## The two likely people

**Greenewald:** His strongest contribution is a reproducible FOIA trail: agency responses, document identifiers, original scans, and a searchable 713-file CIA set. His own release page warns that the CIA's description of the disc as its “entire” collection cannot be independently verified. His NRO release shows interagency UAP reporting, while the redactions sharply limit any inference about intelligence content. [CIA set](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/ufos-the-central-intelligence-agency-cia-collection/) · [NRO FOIA](https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/nro/F-2021-00164.pdf).

**Greer:** His public library labels a category “Disclosure Documents,” and his broader Disclosure Project Intelligence Archive launched in 2024 as a work in progress. Five directly accessible PDFs were saved for comparison. His 99-page, 2001 executive summary organizes witness accounts into pilot/radar, nuclear-site, government-insider, and technology claims, then calls for public and congressional action. Those are **Greer's compiled testimony and interpretations**; the summary alone does not authenticate each event, an alien origin, or a claimed secret energy program. [Greer library](https://drstevengreer.com/document-library/) · [Disclosure archive announcement](https://drstevengreer.com/dpia/).

The companion [[2026-09-25 - CIA UFO records and Greer collection|Greer Clinton collection study]] inventoried all five digitized file units in the [Clinton Presidential Library's FOIA 2006-0483-F finding aid](https://www.clintonlibrary.gov/research/archives/finding-aids/2006-0483-f-steven-greer). It found Greer's submitted advocacy papers, a NASA reply, and two White House scheduling declines; those records establish outreach and official responses, not the alleged secret programs or a presidential meeting. That separate study has the page-specific inventory and source hashes. The finding aid itself warns that this FOIA set is selective.

## Ownership, money, and cross-border links

The Black Vault identifies Greenewald as its founder; the site's donation page says contributions fund hosting, FOIA fees, and storage and says he takes no salary. Its pages also display shop and advertising areas. Those are **self-reported or visible revenue channels**, not audited accounts; beneficial owners, donor amounts, and foreign funding are **unknown** from the reviewed sources. [The Black Vault donation page](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/donate-to-the-black-vault/).

Greer's website carries a **STAR LLC** copyright notice and links to donations, membership, events, and a shop. The archive announcement explicitly seeks crowdfunding. Ownership beyond the website notice, revenue totals, donors, and any cross-border capital are **unknown** from the reviewed sources. International witness stories or a foreign document in an archive are not evidence of foreign financing. [Greer archive announcement](https://drstevengreer.com/dpia/) · [Greer document library](https://drstevengreer.com/document-library/).

## Recommendation and next move

**Start from the original CIA images and the agency document number, then cross-check any extraordinary passage against independent records.** For fresh releases, use NARA's Record Group 615 and AARO's 2026 records/report listings as the update path; neither is part of the 2021 CIA disc. If the founder means Greer, begin with his executive summary's named witnesses and seek independent primary records for one case at a time. Do not merge his advocacy archive with Greenewald's FOIA release merely because both use the word “disclosure.” [NARA collection](https://www.archives.gov/research/topics/uaps/rg-615) · [AARO records](https://www.aaro.mil/UAP-Records/) · [AARO congressional products](https://www.aaro.mil/Congressional-Press-Products/).
