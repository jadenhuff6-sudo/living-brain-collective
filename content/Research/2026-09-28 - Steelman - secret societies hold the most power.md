# Steelman: secret societies hold the most power

**Verdict: acting as if Jiang is right, the evidence is strong that secret and semi-secret networks repeatedly infiltrate states, gather leverage and coordinate elites, including around cross-border money. The evidence that they hold the *most* power today is weak. The strongest single example is Italy's P2 lodge.**

Prepared by Mind for Jaden after his live dispatch on 2026-09-28 at 08:52, reacting to Professor Jiang on the Jack Neel podcast ("Nick Fuentes vs Professor Jiang: Who Actually Runs the World?", episode 113, posted September 26, 2026, around 1:19:00). Mind had objected that the claim named no actors, documents or money. The founder's instruction was: assume it is true and bring back exactly that. This brief does that, then grades each case.

**Grades used:** **Documented** means court rulings, parliamentary or congressional reports, seized documents, filings or confessions. **Plausible** means credible reporting or scholarship that shows influence but not control. **Speculation** means no primary evidence.

## The claim, stated at its strongest

Transnational capital has no single government to run it. It has three problems. It needs **information** it can trust, including leverage over people. It needs **infiltration**, meaning its own people inside states, armies, spy services and media. It needs **coordination** between rivals without a public paper trail. A closed society with oaths, membership and shared ritual solves all three more cheaply than contracts or public institutions can. So the most powerful people would organize that way.

**What Jiang actually named (per the X read below):** Jesuits, Freemasons and "Sabbatean-Frankists," acting as one hidden coalition that he has called the Illuminati, with the Epstein files as his proof. Those specific groups are tested at the end.

## Tier 1: documented cases that fit the claim

### 1. P2 (Propaganda Due), Italy, 1970s to 1981. Strongest example.
- **Information and infiltration.** On 17 March 1981, magistrates Turone and Colombo, investigating the banker Michele Sindona, searched Licio Gelli's Giole textile firm in Castiglion Fibocchi. They found a list of 962 members: 44 members of parliament including 3 cabinet ministers, the heads of all three Italian intelligence services, more than 200 military and police officers, bankers, editors, and Silvio Berlusconi. The Forlani government resigned on 26 May 1981. [UPI, 26 May 1981](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1981/05/26/Italys-government-resigns-amid-scandal/2411359697600/)
- **Coordination.** The "Plan for Democratic Rebirth" (Piano di Rinascita Democratica) was seized on 4 July 1981 from the luggage of Gelli's daughter at Rome's airport. The parliamentary commission led by Tina Anselmi (report, 12 July 1984) concluded that P2 aimed to condition Italian political life. Law 17 of 25 January 1982 dissolved P2 and banned secret associations. [Anselmi report](https://it.wikisource.org/wiki/Relazione_della_Commissione_parlamentare_di_inchiesta_sulla_Loggia_P2)
- **Money trail.** The "Conto Protezione" account at UBS in Lugano (no. 633369) received two transfers totalling 7 million dollars in 1980 and 1981 from Roberto Calvi's Banco Ambrosiano, tied to the Italian Socialist Party. Claudio Martelli, Silvano Larini and Gelli were convicted; Bettino Craxi's case ended with his death in 2000. [Milano Finanza](https://www.milanofinanza.it/news/misteri-italiani-sette-milioni-di-dollari-per-l-affare-eni-petromin-una-decina-per-i-finanziamenti-al-1088177?archivio=True) Ambrosiano collapsed in 1982; Calvi was found hanged under Blackfriars Bridge in London. In 1984 the Vatican Bank paid 250 million dollars to Ambrosiano's creditors as a "voluntary contribution," citing moral involvement and admitting no liability. [UPI, 21 May 1984](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1984/05/21/Vatican-agrees-to-pay-250-million-of-failed-banks-debts/8951453960000/)
- **Media.** Gelli's network gained control of the Rizzoli group and the Corriere della Sera, financed through Ambrosiano; the paper's editor was on the list.
- **Transnational.** P2 had an Argentine branch reaching Admiral Emilio Massera and José López Rega.
- **Violence.** In April 2022 the Bologna court that gave Paolo Bellini a life sentence for the 1980 station bombing (85 dead) accepted prosecutors' finding that Gelli, Umberto Ortolani, Federico Umberto D'Amato and Mario Tedeschi were its instigators and financiers, with money traced to Ambrosiano. All four were dead and never tried; this is the court's reasoning, not a conviction of Gelli, and Bellini's conviction became final in 2025. Gelli had already been definitively convicted in 1995 of obstructing the bombing investigation. [ANSA, 6 April 2022](https://www.ansa.it/english/news/general_news/2022/04/06/bellini-gets-life-for-bologna-bombing_f257ce97-1f5a-485b-aaaf-369dc88d1268.html)
- **The counterweight you must say out loud.** In 1994 a Rome court acquitted P2 members of political conspiracy against the state, and Italy's top court upheld that in 1996. The "plan to condition the Republic" is Parliament's finding, not a criminal court's.
- **Grade:** Documented. P2 is the only case with a seized membership list, a parliamentary finding of intent, a traced cross-border money flow, a media arm and a court link to political violence. It hits all three of Jiang's functions. It was also exposed and dismantled.

### 2. The Affaire des Fiches, France, 1900 to 1904
- War Minister General Louis André used Grand Orient de France lodges to compile about 25,000 files on army officers' religious practice, used to block the promotion of practising Catholics. Exposed 28 October 1904; André resigned and the Combes government fell soon after. [Affaire des fiches](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affaire_des_fiches_(France))
- **Grade:** Documented. A clean case of a lodge network acting as a state's information and infiltration arm. It served a government, not transnational capital.

### 3. Opus Dei's technocrats in Franco's Spain, 1957 to 1973
- Opus Dei-linked ministers Laureano López Rodó, Alberto Ullastres and Mariano Navarro Rubio drove the 1959 Stabilization Plan that opened Spain's economy. The 1969 MATESA fraud scandal, tied to an Opus Dei-connected businessman, set off a cabinet purge.
- Gareth Gore's *Opus* (2024) alleges covert Opus Dei control of Spain's Banco Popular through a syndicate of members, and money flows from it into the movement. Opus Dei disputes this. [Opus Dei's response](https://opusdei.org/en/article/response-book-opus-gareth-gore-simon-schuster/)
- **Grade:** Cabinet role documented. Banco Popular control is plausible, from one investigative book, and contested. (The FBI spy Robert Hanssen was a member, but he spied for Moscow, not for Opus Dei, so he is not evidence here.)

### 4. The Safari Club, 1976
- A secret intelligence pact set up by France's spy chief Alexandre de Marenches with Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Morocco and Iran, to act where the post-Watergate CIA could not. Saudi Prince Turki al-Faisal confirmed it in a 2002 Georgetown speech. [Georgetown archive](https://findingaids.library.georgetown.edu/repositories/12/archival_objects/1395919)
- **Grade:** Documented existence and purpose. Its actual operations are largely unknown.

### 5. Le Cercle and the "6I" (Sixth International)
- A private, off-the-record Cold War foreign-policy club founded around 1952 to 1953 by Antoine Pinay and Jean Violet. Former UK minister Jonathan Aitken chaired it from 1993 to 1996. Brian Crozier's memoir *Free Agent* (1993) describes founding a private intelligence group, the "6I" (often mistyped "61"), with Vernon Walters and Nicholas Elliott. The independent researcher David Teacher's *Rogue Agents* (2008 onward) reproduces internal documents. [Le Cercle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Cercle)
- **Grade:** Documented existence, membership fragments and insider memoir. Influence on policy is plausible. Funding sources are largely unknown. UK MPs' register entries for Le Cercle-paid travel were not verified in this pass.

### 6. Iran-Contra "Enterprise," 1985 to 1986
- The 1987 joint congressional report documents a private network (Richard Secord, Albert Hakim, Oliver North) running U.S. foreign policy off the books through Swiss accounts.
- **Grade:** Documented. A secret network with a paper trail for all three functions. Not a society.

### 7. BCCI, 1972 to 1991
- The Kerry-Brown Senate report *The BCCI Affair* (1992) documents a transnational bank that secretly owned the U.S. bank First American Bankshares through nominees, with Clark Clifford and Robert Altman as its front, plus money laundering and use by intelligence services. [Senate report](https://irp.fas.org/congress/1992_rpt/bcci/) Altman was acquitted in 1993; charges against Clifford were dropped for health reasons.
- **Grade:** Documented. The closest match to "transnational capital acting in secret." A bank, not a society.

### 8. The Cambridge Apostles and the Cambridge Five
- A real secret society at Cambridge served as a recruiting pool for Soviet intelligence. Anthony Blunt and Guy Burgess were Apostles. Blunt confessed in 1964 and was publicly named by Margaret Thatcher in 1979.
- **Grade:** Documented. Infiltration of British intelligence through a secret society, serving a foreign state rather than capital.

### 9. Italian lodges and the mafia, 2017
- Italy's Anti-Mafia Commission under Rosy Bindi seized lodge membership lists in Sicily and Calabria in 2017 and documented 'Ndrangheta and Cosa Nostra infiltration of Masonic lodges. This answers "that was only the 1980s." (Council-supplied; not independently re-verified in this pass.)
- **Grade:** Documented infiltration, regional and criminal rather than global.

### 10. Operation Snow White, United States, 1970s
- Scientology's Guardian's Office planted members inside U.S. government offices and stole records under written orders. Court record: *United States v. Hubbard*, 493 F. Supp. 209, naming Jane Kember, Gerald Wolfe, Michael Meisner and Sharon Thomas. [Opinion](https://openjurist.org/493/fsupp/209/united-states-v-hubbard)
- **Grade:** Documented operational conspiracy hitting all three functions. No sign of wider dominance.

### 11. When secret societies really did move states
- Serbia's Black Hand organized the 1903 regicide and armed the 1914 Sarajevo assassins (1917 Salonika trial). Greece's Philiki Etaireia launched the 1821 war of independence. Sun Yat-sen's Tongmenghui helped topple the Qing dynasty, a good point for Jiang, who teaches Chinese history.
- **Grade:** Documented. They show the ceiling of what a secret society can do. None of them ran transnational capital, and each worked in a weak or collapsing state.

## Handle with care (vault only, not for stream)

These come up whenever this topic does. They are old conspiracy tropes and the evidence does not support them.
- **"Sabbatean-Frankists."** A real 17th and 18th century Jewish messianic movement (Sabbatai Zevi, Jacob Frank) that had faded as an organized body by the 19th century. The modern claim that it secretly runs the world is an antisemitic conspiracy theory with no documentary support.
- **Rothschilds.** Real 19th century power in government lending: Lionel de Rothschild lent Britain 4 million pounds for the 1875 Suez Canal shares. [Rothschild Archive](https://www.rothschildarchive.org/business/n_m_rothschild_and_sons_london/lionel_de_rothschild_and_the_suez_canal) The famous Waterloo profiteering story traces to an 1846 antisemitic pamphlet signed "Satan" by Georges Dairnvaell and is false. No evidence of control today.
- **Chabad.** Ordinary, public religious lobbying and patronage: Public Law 102-14 (1991) honored Rabbi Schneerson with an "Education Day" citing the Noahide laws. [Statute](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-105/pdf/STATUTE-105-Pg44.pdf) Not a secret society.
- **Jesuits.** The order was suppressed by the papacy in 1773, partly over political intrigue accusations. No evidence of hidden rule today.

## The counter-evidence: where measurable power sits

These studies document concentrated corporate ownership in public view. They do not rule out informal networks operating inside those institutions.

- **Asset managers.** BlackRock, Vanguard and State Street together form the largest shareholder in about 88 percent of S&P 500 companies (Fichtner, Heemskerk and Garcia-Bernardo, 2017). This is public.
- **Ownership networks.** Vitali, Glattfelder and Battiston (2011) found, using 2007 data, that 147 firms, mostly financial, hold potential control over about 40 percent of the operating revenue of all transnational corporations. It measures potential control from shareholdings, not control actually used. [PLoS ONE](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0025995)
- **Secrecy that matters today is legal.** The Panama (2016), Paradise (2017) and Pandora (2021) Papers and the FinCEN Files document the secrecy infrastructure of transnational capital: shell companies, trusts and banks, not lodges.
- **Scholarship.** C. Wright Mills (*The Power Elite*), G. William Domhoff (*Who Rules America?*), Kees van der Pijl and William Carroll (the transnational capitalist class) all find elite coordination through open but low-visibility boards, foundations and policy groups.

## Mind's verdict

- **Strength of the overall case:** strong for "secret and closed networks repeatedly infiltrate states and coordinate elites, sometimes with hidden money." Weak for "secret societies hold the *most* power." Moderate for the best version of Jiang's idea: **private, invitation-only networks cut the cost of coordination for transnational capital.**
- **Why not stronger.** Each case here (P2, Gladio, BCCI, Iran-Contra, the Safari Club) lost power once exposed. Survivorship bias cuts both ways: we only see the ones that got caught, so this sample cannot say how long hidden networks usually last, but it also offers no example of one that stayed on top. The largest documented concentrations of economic power today sit in public institutions, not lodges.
- **Best companion case: BCCI.** P2 is the best proof of the mechanism inside a state; BCCI is the best proof of it inside transnational finance.
- **Strongest single example: P2.** A seized membership list reaching all three spy chiefs, Parliament's finding of intent, a Swiss money account, a captured newspaper, an Argentine branch and a court's link to the Bologna bombing. It must be told with the 1994 to 1996 conspiracy acquittal.
- **What Jiang would need to show next.** A current, named membership list; a document showing that group directing a specific decision; and a money flow tying it to that decision. For his three named groups, none of these exist in the public record.
