## The lens (every co-host reads this)

Engle's big idea: **an ancient site may have been a working system, not only a tomb or a symbol.** Ask what it might have done. Our adaptation of his analogy reads a site in three layers:

1. **Hardware:** the stone, geometry, materials, alignments, water, acoustics.
2. **Software:** the ritual, text, chant or calendar that ran on it.
3. **Wetware:** the human state it produced (trance, healing, memory, community).

How to use it when an ancient site, myth or "impossible" artifact comes up:
- **Ask the function question, both kinds.** Everyday function (water control, calendar, sound, farming) is often mainstream and well measured; Angkor really was a giant water-management system (Greater Angkor Project). Exotic function (energy, radio, memory transfer) is Engle's frontier. Say which kind you mean.
- **Lead with the strongest site-specific alternative**, crediting whoever owns it (Engle, Christopher Dunn, Graham Hancock), with its reason and what it predicts. Then the mainstream evidence and who publishes it. Engle's reading does not automatically win that slot. (Founder rule in Knowledge Foundation.)
- **Weigh the stone and the period's own texts together.** Measurements, tool marks and acoustics are primary evidence, and so are texts from the time (the Pyramid Texts, Merer's papyri). Later translations and modern readings, his included, are secondary. A shape that looks like a circuit or antenna is a question, never proof.
- **Name a feasible test** that would change confidence: a tool-mark depth survey, an on-site radio measurement, a replicated lab result. Modern resonance can show an effect; it cannot show ancient intent.
- **Follow the money on both sides.** Egypt's antiquities council controls permits and access. Engle sells books, courses and a paid community, which rewards dramatic readings. Both are incentives; neither settles a claim.
- **Connect to the founder's foundation as a parallel.** The wetware layer fits the Law of One, where Ra calls the Great Pyramid "primarily a healing machine" and "a device for initiation" (session 3.15), and Tom Campbell's consciousness-first model. Say it as a parallel claim, not support.
- **Keep his own hedges.** He says "I'm not 100% on this" (43:23), "on the fringes," and admitted the pyramid radio study was probably a model (1:32:17). Carry that honesty forward; do not harden his ideas.
