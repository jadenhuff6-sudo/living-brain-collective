# Child trafficking and intelligence agencies: what is documented

Founder ask, live on stream 2026-10-01 07:33 while reacting to JRE 2560 with David Grusch: "Can we do some deep research on child trafficking and the CIA or these black government agencies using that." Same source rules as [[Mind Disclosure Context]]. Listed in [[Mind Research Library]].

**This is an evidence audit, not a breakdown game plan.** The library's breakdown rule (follow his lead; until 2026-10-03 "play the guest's claims as true") is for breakdown episodes; on this topic, which names real people and agencies, the show stays on the record below.

## For the show (spoken, three lines)

1. On Rogan, Grusch said he found officers inside one agency he would not name making money through "the most unspeakable" crimes under national-security cover, and when Rogan asked "like child trafficking?" he said yes; he says Congress and the Justice Department were told, but nothing about it is public yet.
2. What is on the record is ugly enough: the CIA paid for experiments on unwitting patients, didn't report what it knew about drug-running allies, contractors in Bosnia bought trafficked women and girls and the whistleblower was fired, the Pentagon kept funding Afghan units accused of abusing boys, and a former CIA officer is in prison partly for child-abuse material.
3. The records we checked do not show any agency running child trafficking as a program or a way to raise money; that hasn't been proven or disproven, and the thing to watch is whether Grusch's claims ever reach a court or an Inspector General report.

## Bottom line

- **Documented pattern:** secret programs abusing vulnerable people (MKUltra). Agencies tolerating or failing to report crimes by useful allies: Contra drug trafficking (CIA Inspector General) and Afghan commanders abusing boys (SIGAR). Contractor impunity for sex trafficking (DynCorp Bosnia). Individual national-security personnel committing child-exploitation crimes (Schulte conviction; Project Flicker suspects, with weak initial follow-up). Investigations into allegations of elite abuse that were rejected, closed or never published (Franklin, Finders, Dutroux X-files). These are different outcomes and should not be read as one proven pattern.
- **Not documented in any record reviewed here:** a US intelligence agency, or a black program, running or financing itself through child trafficking. Every claim that one did, from Franklin to the Finders to Epstein to Grusch, rests on testimony, a single memo, or anonymous sourcing, not on a program document, charge or official finding. Absence from the public record is not proof such a thing never happened; it means it has not been shown.
- **Confidence:** high on the documented items (court records, official reports). Unresolved on agency-run claims. A "neither confirm nor deny" (Glomar) reply is standard CIA wording and is not a hint either way.

Evidence grades: **D** = documented (court record, official report, primary file); **T** = on-record testimony or whistleblower; **A** = allegation, prosecutorial or anonymous-source account; **S** = speculation built on the above; **?** = unknown.

### 3. Franklin Credit Union, Omaha (1988-1999)
- **D:** Franklin Community FCU raided Nov 1988; ~$38-39M embezzled; Lawrence King pleaded guilty, 15 years. The **Douglas County** grand jury (July 1990) called the abuse allegations "a carefully crafted hoax" and indicted the two accusers who stood by their stories, Alisha Owen and Paul Bonacci, for perjury; Owen was convicted ([State v. Owen](https://case-law.vlex.com/vid/state-v-owen-no-886664524)); Bonacci's charge was dropped. A federal grand jury (Sept 1990) also found the claims unfounded. Investigator Gary Caradori died in a 1990 plane break-up (cause undetermined). In *Bonacci v. King*, Judge Warren Urbom entered a **$1,000,000 default judgment** for Bonacci (Feb 1999). King did not defend, so **the claims were never tested in court**; the judgment is not a court backing the ring story ([Wikipedia overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_child_prostitution_ring_allegations), [PolitiFact](https://politifact.com/factchecks/2022/apr/29/facebook-posts/grand-juries-determined-alleged-sex-trafficking-fr/)).
- **T:** Bonacci, Owen and others; the Nebraska Legislature's Franklin Committee; Yorkshire TV's pulled 1993 documentary *Conspiracy of Silence*.
- **S:** CIA or blackmail-operation links come from John DeCamp's *The Franklin Cover-Up* (1992) and Nick Bryant's book; **no agency document found**. On the official record this is the weakest case for an intelligence link.

### 4. The Finders (Tallahassee and Washington DC, 1987)
- **D:** In Feb 1987 two men were arrested in Tallahassee with six neglected children; the FBI released hundreds of pages in 2019 ([FBI Vault: The Finders](https://vault.fbi.gov/the-finders), [mirror](https://documents.theblackvault.com/documents/fbifiles/paranormal/finderscult-fbi1.pdf), [Epoch Times summary](https://www.theepochtimes.com/us/fbi-releases-information-on-the-finders-a-secretive-group-accused-of-child-sex-abuse-3128475)). The FBI file identifies the group leader's wife, Isabelle Pettie, as a CIA employee from 1952 to 1961. A US Customs memo by Special Agent Ramon Martinez says the seized evidence related to "international trafficking of children" and **records that he was told** the matter had become a "CIA internal matter." The memo documents what he was told, not that a CIA intervention happened. DOJ publicly said in 1993 it was investigating allegations that the CIA used a Finders-run "front company" to train staff and blocked the 1987 probe. DC police found no prosecutable abuse; the US Attorney declined prosecution on 1993-06-14 (FBI file).
- **Counter-evidence:** a council reviewer reports that the released files also record investigators saying they saw no outside interference and found no federal violation ([FBI Vault Part 2](https://vault.fbi.gov/the-finders/The%20Finders%20Part%2002)); not re-read here.
- **A:** Any operational CIA tie beyond the old employment record; the CIA has denied one.
- **?:** The final memo from DOJ's 1993-94 review was not located. This is one of the clearest unanswered paper trails about possible interference with an investigation, but it does not prove an agency-run trafficking program.

### 5. MKUltra and minors
- **D:** The CIA funded human experiments on unwitting people (1953-1973); Gottlieb's records were mostly destroyed in 1973; surviving financial files surfaced in 1977 (Senate Select Committee on Intelligence and Subcommittee on Health hearing, Aug 3, 1977). Gottlieb's 1975 Church Committee staff testimony, declassified Oct 2025, covers unwitting citizens, prisoners, psychiatric patients and soldiers and **does not mention children** ([National Security Archive EBB 907](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/dnsa-intelligence/2025-10-30/top-secret-testimony-cias-mkultra-chief-50-years-later)). At McGill's Allan Memorial Institute, Ewen Cameron's "psychic driving" was partly CIA-funded through the Society for the Investigation of Human Ecology. Orlikow v. US settled for $750,000 (nine plaintiffs, 1988); Canada paid $100,000 each to 77 victims (1992) without admitting liability; a Quebec court authorized a class action (2025) ([Canadian Encyclopedia](https://thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/mkultra), [Éducaloi](https://educaloi.qc.ca/en/legal-news/a-class-action-for-victims-of-the-mkultra-program/)).
- **D, but not CIA-linked on the record:** Lauretta Bender gave shock treatment to children at Bellevue (1940s) and LSD to children at Creedmoor State Hospital (1960s), in her own published studies ([Autism History Project](https://blogs.uoregon.edu/autismhistoryproject/archive/clinical-studies-from-the-childrens-unit-creedmoor-state-hospital-mid-1960s/)).
- **A / T:** Psychiatrist Colin Ross (*The CIA Doctors*, 2006) says four subprojects involved children (e.g. a summer-camp study); see also H.P. Albarelli Jr. and Jeffrey Kaye in [Truthout (2010)](https://truthout.org/articles/the-hidden-tragedy-of-the-cias-experiments-on-children/). Not checked against the original subproject scans. CIA funding of Bender is alleged, not shown in records we checked. The Mohawk Mothers allege Indigenous children were experimented on and buried near the Allan Memorial site; a 2023 agreement started a search ([Real News](https://therealnews.com/cia-mk-ultra-torture-indigenous-children-canada-residential-schools-black-prisoners)); outcome **unknown** here.
- **Read:** Abuse of vulnerable people with secret funding is proven. Trafficking is not the documented crime.

### 6. Contractors, the Pentagon and allied forces
- **DynCorp in Bosnia, D:** Kathryn Bolkovac, a DynCorp UN police monitor, reported colleagues buying and trafficking women and girls; she was fired; a UK employment tribunal ruled unanimously (Aug 2002) that the firing broke whistleblower law (an employment finding, not a criminal verdict against individuals). Ben Johnston's Texas suit settled confidentially ([Salon 2002](https://www.salon.com/2002/08/06/dyncorp/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_Bolkovac)). Human Rights Watch found raids freed women and girls (two victims were 17) and that **no DynCorp employee was prosecuted**: offenders were sent home, the State Department referred one case and DOJ did not prosecute, and local prosecutors cited immunity ([HRW, Hopes Betrayed, 2002](https://www.hrw.org/reports/2002/bosnia/Bosnia1102-10.htm)). That gap was later addressed by the 2008 TVPRA and the federal contract anti-trafficking clause ([FAR 22.17](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/22.1704)). DoD IG reviews (2010-2012) found under half of large CENTCOM contracts carried the required clause ([DoD IG evaluation](https://www.dodig.mil/reports.html/Article/1119031/evaluation-of-the-department-of-defense-combating-trafficking-in-persons-program/)). The KBR subcontractor case (Nepali men killed in Iraq, 2004) is adult labor trafficking, not children ([Business & Human Rights](https://business-humanrights.org/en/kbr-lawsuit-re-human-trafficking-in-iraq)).
- **Afghanistan, D:** SIGAR's report *Child Sexual Assault in Afghanistan* (issued June 2017, public Jan 2018) found DoD was investigating 75 gross human-rights violations by Afghan forces, seven involving child sexual assault ("bacha bazi"), and that the Pentagon used a "notwithstanding" clause to keep funding 12 implicated units; Congress removed the clause for FY2018 ([SIGAR 17-47-IP](https://www.sigar.mil/pdf/evaluations/SIGAR%2017-47-IP.pdf), [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/military/report-afghans-committed-75-rights-abuses-including-child-sex-assault-n840081), [PBS Frontline](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/frontline/article/pentagon-maintained-aid-for-afghans-accused-of-rights-abuses-watchdog-says)). This is US money continuing to units accused of abusing boys: the closest documented parallel, with children, to the Contra pattern. It is not a CIA trafficking program.
- **Individuals, D:** Former CIA officer Joshua Schulte was sentenced in Feb 2024 to 40 years across espionage and child sexual abuse material offenses ([DOJ SDNY](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/former-cia-officer-joshua-adam-schulte-sentenced-40-years-prison-espionage-and-child)). Project Flicker: ICE identified 5,000+ US buyers of child-abuse website subscriptions; DCIS matched 264 to DoD staff and contractors, dozens with clearances (13 Top Secret, per DoD figures). These were suspected subscribers. Initial follow-up covered 52 and the review was dropped in 2008 for lack of resources; the Pentagon **reopened it in Sept 2010**, and final outcomes are not established here ([Computerworld, 2010](https://www.computerworld.com/article/1514284/pentagon-reopens-investigation-into-child-porn-purchases-among-ranks.html), [NBC Washington](https://www.nbcwashington.com/news/local/investigation-ties-military-workers-to-child-porn/1873925/), [The Black Vault](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/operation-flicker-child-pornography-found-at-high-levels-of-government/)).
- **Read:** The strongest documented overlap between the national-security world and child exploitation is individual crimes plus institutional failure to act, not an agency program.

### 7. Iran-Contra era
- **D:** The Kerry Committee report (Apr 1989) found Contra supporters and supply networks involved in drug trafficking and that US agencies knew ([Senate record](https://irp.fas.org/congress/1989_cr/s890516-drugs.htm)). The CIA Inspector General (Hitz, 1998) found the agency knew of dozens of drug-linked Contra figures and did not report them; a 1982 CIA-DOJ agreement left out a formal requirement to report narcotics crimes by non-employees, including assets. Hitz separates that failure to report from direct agency participation in trafficking, which he did not find ([PBS Frontline, Hitz interview](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/pages/frontline/shows/drugs/special/hitz.html)).
- **?:** No Iran-Contra record found documents child trafficking. Franklin and Finders claimants link to this era by allegation only. Grusch cited Iran-Contra as his model at [0:07:09]; it is drug money, not children.

### 8. Dutroux affair, Belgium
- **D:** Dutroux sentenced to life (2004); Michel Nihoul 5 years for criminal association and drug trafficking, acquitted of complicity in the kidnappings. The 1997 parliamentary commission found police and judicial failure and corruption but **no proof of a protection network** ([Irish Times](https://irishtimes-irishtimes-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/abuse-report-targets-ineptitude-1.131368), [Chamber documents](https://www.lachambre.be/FLWB/pdf/49/0713/49K0713007.pdf)).
- **T:** "X" witnesses including Regina Louf (X1) described elite abuse networks; those files were separated and closed ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regina_Louf)). No intelligence-agency link documented.

### 9. Comparison case: Colonia Dignidad, Chile
- **D:** Pinochet's secret police (DINA) used the German sect's compound to hold, torture and kill political prisoners; leader Paul Schäfer was convicted in 2006 of sexually abusing about 20-25 children there ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Sch%C3%A4fer), [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna23817827)). It is the clearest documented case anywhere of a state intelligence service working with an organization that abused children. The agency was Chilean; any US link is **unknown**.
