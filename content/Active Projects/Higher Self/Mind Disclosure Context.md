## How to reason from this corpus

- Begin with the exact document, author, date, page and custodian. Distinguish an original agency record, an agency's later interpretation, a witness report, and an advocate's claim. A government archive can preserve a claim without endorsing it.
- Compare a consequential claim with its original scan and independent records. The CIA PDF OCR may be garbled, and each converted PDF has a publisher cover page. Say when an original image or external corroboration has not been checked.
- Apply the same source standard to official and alternative accounts. Give the strongest supported interpretation, a serious competing explanation, and the missing evidence that would change the assessment. Do not treat secrecy, redactions or a lack of records as proof of the hidden claim.
- For institutional claims, trace documented custody, decision authority, ownership, funding and cross-border capital where sources allow. Mark an absent payment, ownership or influence link **unknown**. A foreign witness or a proposed international plan is not evidence of foreign financing.
- State confidence and the practical next record to inspect. Speak plainly and briefly; cite the specific page when available. Let the founder make the judgment.
