# Reptilians vs grays: the NHI war theory, built and tested

Founder ask, live on stream 2026-10-01 08:30 while reacting to JRE 2560 with David Grusch: "What research can we do to find this war between the reptilians and the grays, and what is your perspective on why they would be at war? And why they would be doing it near us." Linked from [[2026-10-01 - Grusch JRE 2560 live finds]]; listed in [[Mind Research Library]].

**This is a theory frame.** The strongest case is built first, then tested. Every item carries its kind: **testimony** (someone says it), **channeled** (the Law of One), **myth** (ancient text, an analogy and not evidence), **record** (official document), **speculation** (our own synthesis).

## For the show (spoken, three lines)

1. Grusch says his colleagues told him about a crashed reptilian tic-tac with dead grays beside it, at least a dozen or two "adversarial" crashes, and living reptilians shot after US personnel were killed; he says he did not see any of it himself, and it is not in his sworn testimony.
2. If it is real, the most coherent motive is not our metal or water, it's us: the Law of One describes a running war between service-to-self and service-to-others groups over a planet at its "harvest," with Earth behind a quarantine they can only cross through rare windows.
3. Nothing official backs a war yet, and the old "gray firefight" stories trace back to a known Air Force disinformation case, so the real research is tracing where Grusch's story came from and waiting for a named first-hand witness.

## Bottom line

- No sworn testimony, court record or official document anywhere describes non-human groups fighting each other. The war exists in Grusch's secondhand Rogan account, discredited 1990s lore, channeled material and myth.
- The most useful research move is a **family tree of the claim**: when "reptilians vs grays" first appears, through whom, and whether Grusch's details are new or match the old lore.
- On air: play it as a theory, say "secondhand" out loud, and keep David Icke's reptilian material off the show (see below).

## 2. The strongest case (theory frame)

1. **A cleared insider says it is in the files** (testimony). A former intelligence officer says the conflict is "documented in collection" and was discussed in the most sensitive government settings.
2. **Experiencers describe the two as different beings** (testimony, recovered and conscious memories). John Mack, *Abduction* (1994): reptilian and gray beings appear "during the same abduction" (Carlos, ch. 14; Arthur, ch. 15). David Jacobs, *Walking Among Us* (2015), separates "insectalins" from "reptalins" and reports abductees are frightened by the reptalins ([full text](https://archive.org/details/walking-among-us-the-alien-plan-to-control-humanity-2015-david-jacobs-ph-d)).
3. **Grays as built beings** (testimony, weak). Philip Corso, *The Day After Roswell* (1997, ch. 7), calls the Roswell bodies "humanoid robots rather than life forms." If grays are made, dead grays at a reptilian crash could be crew or equipment of one side.
4. **Ancient faction-and-rebellion stories** (myth, analogy only):
   - **Garuda vs the Nagas**: the bird-king against the serpent race. The serpents' mother Kadru wins Garuda's mother Vinata as a slave by cheating on a bet; Garuda steals the nectar of immortality to free her and asks that "the mighty snakes become my food" (Mahabharata, Adi Parva, Astika section, Ganguli sections 23 to 34, [sacred-texts](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/m01/m01024.htm)). The cleanest reptile-versus-other enmity in old texts; it is about slavery and kin rivalry, not humanity.
   - **Saubha**: King Salwa's flying city besieges Krishna's Dwaraka, a king's revenge (Vana Parva, sections 14 to 21, [sacred-texts](https://sacred-texts.com/hin/m03/m03014.htm)).
   - **The Watchers**: rebel angels descend, take human wives, father giants; the archangels bind them, and Gabriel sets the giants against each other (1 Enoch 6 to 10, R.H. Charles, [ch. 10](https://sacred-texts.com/bib/boe/boe013.htm); compare Genesis 6:1-4). A rebellion and judgment, not a war between equals.
5. **The Law of One gives a framework** (channeled; quoted from the local library, edited edition):
   - A standing conflict: "Battles have been and are continuing to be fought upon these levels" between the Confederation and the Orion Confederation (25.4); "the fourth density of both orientations join in this conflict" (25.10).
   - Earth is quarantined to protect free will (16.1, 16.2). Outsiders get in only through "windows or distortions in the space/time continua... These windows are rare and unpredictable" (12.7).
   - Pressure rises now: "As your harvest approaches, those forces of what you would call light work according to their call. The ones of Orion have the working only according to their call" (24.8). Earth is in "a somewhat difficult harvest" (13.22).
   - Orion's tools: "a thought-form; the second, a kind of robot" (12.18); a "construct" could be held "for a brief period" (12.23).
   - Craft (aside, not a hook): Orion "chariots" include "the elongated, ovoid shape... metallic... in the absence of light, it appears to be red or fiery" among several other shapes (12.2). Loose resemblance to the tic-tac at best.

## 3. Mind's perspective: why they would fight, and why here (speculation)

- **If the reports are accurate, the most coherent motive is competition over people.** A civilization that crosses stars does not need Earth's metal or water; what Earth uniquely has is billions of minds at a turning point. Ra's version is polarity at harvest; Jacobs' is a genetic program. This is our synthesis, not something any source states about reptilians and grays.
- **Why near us, three possible reasons:** (1) the contest is wherever the "harvest" is; (2) a quarantine with few windows would bunch encounters at the border; (3) since 1945 we are also a hazard: the 1950s above-ground tests, the 1958 to 1962 high-altitude tests (Starfish Prime, 1962) and the reported UAP activity at nuclear sites (Robert Salas on Malmstrom, 1967: testimony). Our own check found reports do not cluster at missile bases once population is counted ([[2026-10-01 - AI cross-check challenge - what public data shows about UAP]]).
- **Where Ra cuts against the theory.** Ra describes the conflict as a "thought-war" of minds projecting light, ending in "a stand-off" (25.5, 25.6), and says at the quarantine "there is no confrontation" (12.7). The Confederation side does not do physical combat. Ra never names reptilians or grays; mapping them onto Orion and the Confederation is ours, and a physical crash war does not fit Ra cleanly.
- **Non-war readings of the same crashes:** our own forcing craft down (Grusch's "if we force these craft down"); human-built remote craft (Ra 8.26 to 8.28: "The United States... creates these as a type of weapon," with no occupants, "controlled by computer from a remote source of data"); or one group's crew and constructs dying in the same accident.

## 4. What breaks it

- **Secondhand all the way down.** Grusch did not see the bodies, the craft or the shooting, and the war detail is absent from his 2023 sworn testimony.
- **The official counterweight.** The Pentagon's UAP office found "no empirical evidence" for claims of extraterrestrial technology or reverse engineering ([AARO Historical Record Report Vol. 1, March 2024](https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/AARO_Historical_Record_Report_Vol_1_2024.pdf)). That is a bounded finding about what AARO reviewed, not proof about every classified record. The Schumer-Rounds UAP Disclosure Act defined "non-human intelligence" but says nothing about conflict, and it was cut down to a narrower records-collection section before becoming law in the 2024 defense bill ([amendment text](https://www.democrats.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/uap_amendment.pdf)).
- **The lore came first, and part of it was planted.** The Dulce underground-base story grew from Paul Bennewitz, whom Air Force OSI agent Richard Doty fed disinformation in the early 1980s (Greg Bishop, *Project Beta*, 2005; Mark Pilkington, *Mirage Men*, 2010). Phil Schneider's 1995 lectures added a 1979 firefight with "large Grays"; the sources cited on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Schneider) call it not credible. If Grusch's colleagues are repeating that lore, his account is not independent.
- **The experiencer books show a ranking, not a war.** Jacobs calls reptalins rare and "probably the remnants of the program's earliest beginnings," part of a hierarchy; Mack read these experiences as transformative and did not presume they were physical. Much of this material comes from hypnosis; Clancy et al. (2002) found people reporting abductions are more prone to false-memory errors in the lab ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12150421/)). That does not disprove any one account.
- **The historic "sky battles" have ordinary readings.** The 1561 Nuremberg and 1566 Basel broadsheets are usually read as sun dogs (halo effects) in religious warning pamphlets ([Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1561_celestial_phenomenon_over_Nuremberg), [Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1566_celestial_phenomenon_over_Basel)).
- **What X is saying (chatter, not a source; Grok's read, 2026-10-01).** Believers are tying it to Schneider's Dulce story and the Ummo letters; skeptics call it lore with a clearance attached; no new Pentagon, NASA or White House response.
- **Keep Icke off the show.** David Icke's reptilian theory drew on the forged *Protocols of the Elders of Zion*, and critics read his reptilians as code for Jews ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Icke)).

## 5. What research we can actually do

1. **Family tree of the claim (first, cheap, public).** Date every appearance of a reptilian-versus-gray conflict: Bennewitz and Doty (early 1980s), the Dulce lore, Bill Cooper (1989 to 1991), Schneider (1995), Leonard Stringfield's crash-retrieval *Status Reports* (1978 onward, not checked this pass), Ummo, Grusch (2026). Compare each to Grusch's specifics (tic-tac craft, 7-foot gorilla-skinned reptilians, dead grays, soldiers killed). New details that never appear in the old lore would be the interesting signal.
2. **Incident table for Grusch's own claims.** For each crash he alludes to: date, place, original witness, what was observed, records claimed, independent corroboration. Mostly blanks today; it fills as witnesses speak.
3. **Code the experiencer books (second).** Every reptilian and gray scene in Mack (1994), Jacobs (1998, 2015) and Bullard's folklore work ([JSTOR 540677](https://www.jstor.org/stable/540677)), tagged as together, ranked or in conflict, with hypnosis and prior exposure noted. Tests how common the war story is, not whether a crash happened.
4. **Watch for a named first-hand witness.** Grusch says the people at the reptilian crash may speak (2:08:18). A named, corroborated first-hand account is what would move this from story to testimony. Also watch Rep. Burlison's follow-up and any later AARO volume.
5. **Law of One map (low priority).** The library's "Orion" category tags 59 passages; read them for window mechanics and anything describing physical conflict.
