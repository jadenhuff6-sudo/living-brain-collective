# Fact-check: Springmeier, *Bloodlines of the Illuminati* (1995), "The Bundy Bloodline" (PDF pp. 18-26)

Frame for the episode: **what the book claims vs what the record shows.**
Compiled 2026-10-06. Every URL below was actually opened during research. Anything I could not confirm is marked **Unverified**.
Verdict scale: **Documented** (record supports it) / **Partly** (core is real, details wrong or inflated) / **Unsupported** (no evidence offered or found) / **False** (record contradicts it) / **Unverified** (could not check either way).

Ted Bundy content: no victims named, no graphic detail.

---

## The big picture in one paragraph

The chapter mixes three kinds of material. (1) **Real, checkable résumé facts** about Harvey, William and McGeorge Bundy — Skull and Bones, Stimson, the Manhattan Project, Carnegie, CIA, Vietnam, Ford Foundation — most of which hold up, largely because they are lifted from Antony Sutton's 1983 *America's Secret Establishment*. (2) **Garbled versions of real events** — the McCarthy/Hiss passport episode, Maheu's CIA work, the Angola memo, the Ford resignation — where the record exists but says something different from the book. (3) **Pure assertion with no evidence** — MJ-12, "Eric Bundy," the Hughes kidnapping, McCarthy "killed," Ted Bundy as part of the family, Satanism. The author himself admits (p.19) he found nothing tying Ted Bundy to the political Bundys. The irony for the episode: the documented record of what the Bundy brothers actually did (Vietnam escalation, a war resolution drafted two months before the Tonkin incident, the ghost-written A-bomb defense) is more consequential than anything the book invents — and the book mostly misses it.

## 1. Ted Bundy's "the force" quote / *The Phantom Prince* (1981) p.176

- **Claim (p.18):** After his final Florida arrest, Bundy told girlfriend Elizabeth Kendall by phone that "the force" made him kill; quoted from *The Phantom Prince*, Madrona Publishers, 1981, p.176.
- **Verdict: Partly (substance real; exact wording and page Unverified).**
- **Record:** The book exists (Madrona, 1981; Kendall is the pen name of Elizabeth Kloepfer). Kloepfer has said on camera that in a jailhouse call after his arrest Bundy told her he was "consumed by something that he didn't understand" and couldn't contain it — so the gist is real. I could not open the book's text (Internet Archive copy is lending-restricted), so the exact quote and page 176 are unconfirmed. In his death-row interviews with Michaud and Aynesworth, Bundy called the same thing "the entity," a "malignant being," and blamed pornography — not anything occult.

## 3. Ted Bundy's real origins

- **Claim (p.19):** He was adopted by "his father Johnnie Bundy, who was a dentist"; possibly tied to the elite Bundy line.
- **Verdict: False (dentist); Unsupported (bloodline).**
- **Record:** Born **Theodore Robert Cowell**, Nov. 24, 1946, at the Elizabeth Lund Home for Unwed Mothers in Burlington, Vermont. His mother moved to Tacoma in 1950 and in 1951 married **Johnny Culpepper Bundy, a hospital cook** (Michaud & Aynesworth p.57), who adopted him. "Bundy" is therefore a stepfather's surname, not Ted's blood line — so even a genealogical tie between Johnny Bundy and the Boston Bundys would make Ted no part of any "bloodline." The author himself says (p.19) he found nothing linking them. I found nothing either.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Bundy (birth and stepfather passages cite Michaud & Aynesworth p.57 and Rule) ; https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Ted_Bundy&action=raw

## 10. Harry W. Bundy and the SRICF

- **Claim (pp.21, 25-26):** Harry W. Bundy, "Mason, Satanist," became chief adept (9°) of the SRICF's Colorado college on April 20, 1948; the SRICF/SRIA is a Satanic branch of the Illuminati.

The **SRICF** (U.S. branch since 1880) is an invitation-only study society for Christian Master Masons — its local units are called "colleges" and its grades run up to "Adept." It is the American sister of the English SRIA (founded 1867). Wynn Westcott did lead the SRIA and co-founded the Golden Dawn, which is a real historical overlap with occultism. But "Satanist" is the author's label, not something the record shows.

## Documented Bundy-family facts the book missed (strongest material for the episode)

1. **A war resolution written before the incident.** A "Draft Congressional Resolution" giving the president broad Southeast Asia war powers, dated June 11, 1964, was prepared in William Bundy's Far East bureau and circulated by McGeorge Bundy — two months before the Gulf of Tonkin incident. LBJ put it aside, then used the idea in August. Source: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v01/d214
2. **The ghost-written A-bomb defense.** The famous Feb. 1947 *Harper's* article "The Decision to Use the Atomic Bomb," under Stimson's name, was largely written by McGeorge Bundy from his father Harvey's memo, on Conant's suggestion and edited by Gen. Groves. It shaped the American story of Hiroshima for decades. Source: https://www.historynewsnetwork.org/article/why-americans-have-been-duped-over-the-use-of-the-
3. **The Vietnam escalation memos.** Jan. 27, 1965 "fork in the road" memo (with McNamara): current policy "can lead only to disastrous defeat… Bob and I tend to favor" using military power. Feb. 7, 1965, after Pleiku: recommended "sustained reprisal" against North Vietnam, putting the odds of success at "somewhere between 25% and 75%." Sources: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v02/d42 ; https://vassar.edu/the-wars-for-vietnam/documents/excerpts-mcgeorge-bundys-memo-president-johnson-february-7-1965
4. **NSAM 273 drafted the day before Dallas.** McGeorge Bundy wrote the first draft of NSAM 273 on Nov. 21, 1963; the version LBJ approved on Nov. 26 widened planning for covert action against the North. Source: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v04/d331
5. **The CIA "executive action" briefing.** Early in the Kennedy years, CIA's Richard Bissell briefed McGeorge Bundy on an "executive action" capability — a term that included assassination — though not on the Castro plot specifically (Church Committee report p.121). Source: https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/sites/default/files/94465.pdf
6. **Cuban Missile Crisis, day one.** McGeorge Bundy is the one who told JFK about the U-2 missile photos, at 8:45 a.m., Oct. 16, 1962. He had waited overnight so the president could get some rest. Source: https://microsites.jfklibrary.org/cmc/oct16/doc2.html
7. **The "Harrison-Bundy Files."** The Army's core Manhattan Project policy files carry Harvey Bundy's name — a real paper trail at the center of the atomic program, more concrete than anything the book claims. Source: https://history.aip.org/catalog/icos/4042.html

## Where the chapter's material comes from

- Most Harvey/William/McGeorge material, the "23-year-old" quote, the Ford resignation line, Howe, National Goals, and the Angola memo are taken from **Antony Sutton, *America's Secret Establishment* (1983)**, often word for word, with the interpretation pushed further.
- The Hughes kidnapping, Onassis and doubles story comes from the **Gemstone File** (1970s).
- MJ-12 comes from the author's own earlier book; the MJ-12 papers are judged fake.
- Ted Bundy is linked to the family only by surname — a stepfather's surname.
