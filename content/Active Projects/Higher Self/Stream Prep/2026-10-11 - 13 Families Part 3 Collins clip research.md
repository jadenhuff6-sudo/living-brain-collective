# 13 Families, Part 3: the Collins family: stream clip list

Pulled 2026-10-10 for the Sunday 2026-10-11 stream. Every range below was checked against the video's YouTube captions, downloaded with yt-dlp into the session scratchpad (`scratchpad/collins/subs/`) and converted to timestamped .txt files. Times are caption-cue times, so give each one a second or two either side. "First" and "last" are the caption words at the range edges. Auto-captions mangle names in places; corrections are in brackets.

Views are as of the pull date. "Re-upload" means the channel is not the original rights holder.

**The chapter in one line, for context:** Springmeier rates his own Collins research "about at 10" out of 100 (p. 27). His named source for the Collins bloodline is John Todd, whose witch name was "Lance Collins" (see the companion [[2026-10-11 - 13 Families Part 3 Collins fact-check]]). So the strongest clips here are Todd in his own voice, then the record on Todd, then the real Collins names the book leans on.

  - Stop at 01:07. Right after, he starts on his "shooting incident" story.

**1C. The sympathetic retelling (with Todd tape audio inside)**: Taylor Alesia, "The man who woke up millions of Christians in 1972" (about 356K views)

  - Use this for how the story spreads today (356K views), not as a source.

  - Key lines:

    - "In 1988 Todd was convicted in South Carolina on charges of rape and sentenced to 30 years."
- **Range B (Bonewits investigated him; the Illuminati claims; Falwell): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f69TM9fUPw8&t=210 — 03:30–05:01**
  - First: "following an investigation of his activities by neo-pagan leaders Isaac Bonewits and Gavin Frost..."
  - Last: "...he claimed that Falwell had been bribed by the Illuminati with a 50 million dollar donation."
  - This is the big catch: **Isaac Bonewits**, whom the book places on the "Grand Druid Council" (p. 37), is the man who investigated Todd's coven.
    - Bonewits's investigation turned up drug use and underage sex.
    - The coven's Wiccan charter was revoked.
    - Todd was convicted of contributing to the delinquency of a minor.
  - Also covers Todd's late-1977 Illuminati claims:
    - Falwell, Billy Graham and Oral Roberts were in on it.
    - He paid $8 million to launch Christian rock.
    - Carter was the Antichrist.
  - Tie-in to the book: the Falwell "bribe" is the same thread as the book's "Yvonne Collins got the SEC to investigate Falwell."
  - The caption at 02:56 ("created his wife's teenage sister") is garbled; skip that line.
