# Community visual vault vision

## Founder request
Source: direct founder message in Codex, October 7, 2026. Wants a visual, interactive vault of his theory of everything that his community can explore and learn from, supporting Substack and other content. Named spirituality, science, history, how the world works, mysticism, occultism, Hermeticism, spiritual practices, religion, light/dark, military/technocratic/financial industrial complexes, “13 families,” the devil, devil worship, extreme allegations including cannibalism, spiritual evolution, and the Law of One.

This records requested coverage, not assent to every claim or proof of any allegation. The central explanatory thesis remains to be elicited; do not invent founder doctrine.

## Existing foundation and limits
Continue [[Theory of Everything Map]], not a duplicate research system. Its October 1 overview reports a built pilot and explicitly warns that 33 claims were checked against crew summaries rather than original records. The code directory `[local path removed]` exists today. No research back-fill, claim revalidation, or live crew integration performed this session. The existing foundation's source/claim distinction, corrections, and named-actor safeguards remain intact. See Knowledge Foundation.

## Proposed community layer, not approved architecture
Living atlas with six navigable gateways: consciousness/identity; traditions/cosmology; science/reality; practice/experience; history/power; light/shadow/contested claims. Spiritual evolution is a proposed visual center, not a founder-approved master thesis. Community map edges must identify a relationship, analogy, or hypothesis; mere proximity is not evidence of causation.

Topic pages should distinguish founder interpretation, primary source passages with exact locators, empirical findings, attributed teachings, personal experience, contested allegations, competing explanations, and practical reflections. No unsupported aggregate-family culpability or named-person accusation. History and institutional evidence should not be collapsed into a religious account, or vice versa.

Content flow proposed: private raw sources -> reviewed topic/claim pages -> public atlas <-> Substack essays and videos. Start with a small coherent guided path, not a sprawling graph. Public release/hosting, costs and founder review remain separate.
