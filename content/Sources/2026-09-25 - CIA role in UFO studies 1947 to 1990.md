# CIA role in UFO studies, 1947–1990

## Summary

This 1997 CIA historical study recounts substantial agency concern in the early 1950s, CIA sponsorship of the 1953 Robertson Panel, later secrecy about that sponsorship, and a 1978 Freedom of Information Act release. It says the 1978 search found 355 records of about 900 pages and released all but 57 records of about 100 pages at the time. Its account found no proof of extraterrestrial visits in those records. This is an agency retrospective, which should be checked against underlying documents for disputed details.
