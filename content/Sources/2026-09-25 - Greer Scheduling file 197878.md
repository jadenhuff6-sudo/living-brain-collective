# Greer disclosure packet and scheduling decline

## Summary

Forty-one PDF pages: archive markers and redaction sheets; a December 6, 1996 White House scheduling decline; Greer’s November 15, 1996 letter and attached CSETI position papers; and a second Greer letter seeking a meeting. The extraterrestrial, covert-program and witness claims are Greer-authored allegations, not agency findings.
