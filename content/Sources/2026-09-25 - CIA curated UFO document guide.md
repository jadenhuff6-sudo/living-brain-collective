# CIA curated UFO document guide

## Summary

The CIA's 2016 public guide points to ten historical UFO files, arranged as five that might intrigue a believer and five that invite skeptical interpretation. The guide is a reading list, not an assessment that any file proves extraterrestrial origin. As checked on 2026-09-25, each linked legacy PDF redirected to the CIA reading-room home page, so these ten are cataloged below as **pointers, not locally ingested document contents**.

## Ten listed records

1. [Flying Saucers Reported Over East Germany, 1952](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/DOC_0000015464.pdf)
2. [Minutes of Branch Chief's Meeting on UFOs, 11 August 1952](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/DOC_0000015441.pdf)
3. [Flying Saucers Reported Over Spain and North Africa, 1952](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/DOC_0000015465.pdf)
4. [Survey of Flying Saucer Reports, 1 August 1952](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/DOC_0000015341.pdf)
5. [Flying Saucers Reported Over Belgian Congo Uranium Mines, 1952](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/DOC_0000015463.pdf)
6. [Scientific Advisory Panel on Unidentified Flying Objects, 14–17 January 1953](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/DOC_0000015458.pdf)
7. [Office Memorandum on Flying Saucers, 15 March 1949](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/DOC_0000015337.pdf)
8. [Memorandum to the Director of Central Intelligence on Flying Saucers, 2 October 1952](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/DOC_0000015339.pdf)
