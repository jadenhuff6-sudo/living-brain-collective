# FBI Hottel memo (1950 New Mexico saucers)

Pulled live on stream 2026-10-05 while watching Jesse Michels' Aztec UFO episode. PDF: !FBI Hottel memo 1950-03-22.pdf

- One-page memo, 22 March 1950, from Guy Hottel (SAC, FBI Washington field office) to the Director.
- Relays a third-hand informant claim: three saucers recovered in New Mexico, each with three bodies about 3 ft tall in metallic cloth; ascribes the downing to high-powered radar.
- The FBI did not investigate or confirm it; the memo only records what it was told. The FBI's own vault page says it is the most viewed file there.
- Commonly linked to the Aztec, NM (Hart Canyon, 1948) story spread by Silas Newton and Leo GeBauer, later discredited as con men (1952 True magazine, J.P. Cahn). Researchers (e.g. Scott Ramsey, *The Aztec Incident*) later gathered local witness accounts such as Doug Noland.
