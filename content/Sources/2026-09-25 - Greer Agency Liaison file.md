# NASA reply and 1996 UFO fact sheet

## Summary

Five PDF pages: one administrative marker, NASA’s July 17, 1996 response to Greer, and a three-page NASA fact sheet. NASA says Greer’s May 23 letter to Clinton was referred for reply and states its 1996 position that it had no evidence tying UFOs to aliens. Referral does not show Clinton personally read the letter.
