# Contributing

Short version: bring a source, say what kind of claim it is, keep private information out, and expect a review before anything is merged.

## Before you open anything

- Search existing issues and the pages in `content/` so you do not repeat what is already there.
- Have at least one source ready. Link it, and say where in it to look (page number, timestamp, section).

## Issues

Use the form that fits: Theory, Cover this on the show, or Critique of a narrative. The forms ask for the claim, the sources, and how sure you are.

## Pull requests

- Add or fix one page per pull request where you can.
- Keep each page's existing format. Pages are plain Markdown.
- Put your sources in the page itself, not only in the pull request.
- Do not edit `EXPORT-MANIFEST.md` or anything under `tools/` unless the pull request is about the export itself.
- Pages in `content/` are overwritten when the export is refreshed from the vault. Accepted changes are carried back into the vault by the maintainer so they are not lost.

## What gets declined

- No source, or a source that does not say what the claim says.
- Private information about a person.
- Personal attacks, harassment, or calls for harm.
- Medical, legal or financial advice presented as fact.

## Review

Every contribution is reviewed by the maintainer. The `main` branch is protected, so nothing merges without that review.
